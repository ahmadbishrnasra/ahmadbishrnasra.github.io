About

A website for positioning, not just listing.

My work sits at the intersection of clinical research, public health, medical education, and quantitative analysis. I am particularly interested in research that is methodologically rigorous, clinically meaningful, and relevant to underrepresented or resource-limited settings.

Beyond authorship and analysis, I care about research development and research culture: helping shape stronger studies, improving how results are communicated, and supporting students and junior researchers as they enter academic work.

What I do

Four parts of my work

Research Development

From question formulation and literature review to protocol planning, questionnaire design, ethics preparation, and study workflows.

Statistical Analysis

SPSS-based data cleaning, analysis, interpretation, and preparation of publication-ready tables, figures, and results narratives.

Scientific Writing

Abstracts, manuscripts, revisions, response-to-reviewer support, reporting standards, and journal submission preparation.

Mentorship & Research Education

Journal club teaching, project consultations, practical research guidance, and support for student researchers entering collaborative work.

Teaching, mentorship & research advocacy

Helping students move from interest to implementation

Through the Damascus University Journal Club and Medical Research Empowerment (MRE), I support early-career researchers and medical students in understanding research methodology, critically reading literature, initiating projects, and participating more confidently in collaborative academic work.

Journal Club

Critical appraisal, evidence-based medicine, research education, and structured discussion of scientific literature.

MRE

Research guidance, collaboration support, educational activities, and pathways for students looking to enter active projects.

Research & statistical support

Open to selected academic collaboration and consultation

I am available to contribute to selected medical and public-health research projects, particularly those needing support in methodology, statistical analysis, scientific writing, or student research mentoring.

I approach this work as academic collaboration and research support rather than a generic freelance service. The goal is thoughtful, rigorous, and publication-oriented work that genuinely strengthens a project.

  • Study design and protocol support
  • Questionnaire-based and observational research consultation
  • SPSS data analysis and interpretation
  • Publication-ready results tables and figures
  • Abstract, poster, and manuscript support
  • Student research mentorship and project consultation
