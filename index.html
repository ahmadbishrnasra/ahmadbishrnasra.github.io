About

Clinical questions.
Rigorous methods.
Meaningful evidence.

My research experience spans the full research process, from study design and multicentre coordination to statistical analysis, interpretation, and scientific reporting. I have performed statistical analyses for more than 60 medical research projects and contributed to 24 peer-reviewed journal articles.

My current clinical interests centre on oncology, particularly cancer prevention, early detection, and improving patient outcomes. Alongside this focus, I have contributed to research across a broad range of medical and public-health topics.

Beyond my own research, I am involved in research education and mentorship through the Damascus University Journal Club and Medical Research Empowerment, supporting students and early-career researchers in developing their methodological and scientific skills.

Selected research

Methods behind the manuscripts

Oncology · Population study

Skin Cancer Prevention & Early Detection

First and corresponding author and sole statistical analyst of a study involving 1,876 adults, with prespecified multivariable models and sensitivity analyses. Preliminary findings appeared as an ASCO 2026 meeting abstract.

Prospective cohort · Surgical outcomes

National Multicentre Surgical Outcomes Cohort

Study lead and sole analyst for a prospective cohort across eight Syrian hospitals, coordinating data collection and analyses of postoperative outcomes with longitudinal methods.

Multicentre study · Medical education

Career Regret & Health-Related Quality of Life

Statistical lead and sole analyst for a study of 1,702 Syrian medical students, including psychometric assessment, robust regression, model diagnostics, and mixed-model sensitivity analysis.
