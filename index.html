About

Medicine, research, and evidence-based practice

I am a final-year MD candidate at the Faculty of Medicine, Damascus University, with active involvement in clinical and population-based research. My work spans observational studies, multicenter coordination, questionnaire-based research, clinical case research, scientific writing, and statistical analysis.

I am particularly interested in clinically relevant research that addresses evidence gaps in Syria and other resource-limited settings, while strengthening rigorous study design, transparent analysis, and clear scientific communication.
