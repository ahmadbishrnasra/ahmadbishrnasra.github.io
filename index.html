About

Clinical questions, quantitative methods, meaningful evidence.

My work is increasingly centred on clinical epidemiology, quantitative clinical research, and biostatistical methods. I am interested in study design, robust analysis, outcomes research, and the generation of evidence that is both methodologically defensible and clinically useful.

My principal clinical interests are oncology and gastroenterology, particularly GI oncology. Alongside my own projects, I collaborate on multicentre and multinational studies, support research teams with methodological and statistical work, and contribute to research education and mentorship.

Selected quantitative work

Methodological depth behind the publication list

Prospective cohort

National Multicentre Surgical Outcomes Cohort

Study lead, central coordinator, statistical lead and sole analyst for a prospective cohort across eight public hospitals. Coordinated 24 data collectors; 405 patients were enrolled, with 389 adults included in the primary analysis.

Population study

Skin Cancer Prevention & Early Detection

First and corresponding author, statistical lead and sole analyst for a study of 1,876 adults, with prespecified multivariable analyses, robust inference, and sensitivity analyses. Preliminary findings were presented at ASCO 2026.

Multicentre education research

Career Regret & Health-Related Quality of Life

Second author, statistical lead and sole analyst for a multicentre study of 1,702 medical students, including psychometric assessment, HC3 robust inference, diagnostics, sensitivity analyses, and university-level mixed modelling.

Research & statistical support

Open to selected academic collaboration and methodological consultation

I am available to contribute to selected clinical and public-health research projects, particularly those needing support in study design, data management, statistical analysis, interpretation, scientific reporting, or research-team development.

I approach this work as academic collaboration and methodological support rather than generic freelance analysis. The goal is rigorous, transparent, publication-oriented work that strengthens the study from question to final report.

  • Study design, protocol, outcome definition, and analytic planning
  • Data cleaning, restructuring, coding, and quality-control workflows
  • Regression modelling, mixed models, GEE, diagnostics, and sensitivity analyses
  • SPSS-based analysis, interpretation, and publication-ready statistical outputs
  • Manuscript, abstract, poster, tables, figures, and peer-review responses
  • Research-methodology consultation and junior-researcher mentorship
